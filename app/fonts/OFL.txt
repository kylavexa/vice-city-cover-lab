Fonts in this folder (anton.woff2, bebas-neue.woff2, pacifico.woff2) are
the SIL Open Font License versions of Anton, Bebas Neue and Pacifico,
downloaded from Google Fonts and shipped here so the app works offline.
See https://openfontlicense.org for the license text.

- Anton (version 27, OFL) — the big poster title.
- Bebas Neue (version 16, OFL) — the letterspaced kicker.
- Pacifico (version 23, OFL) — the "Vice City" script flourish.
